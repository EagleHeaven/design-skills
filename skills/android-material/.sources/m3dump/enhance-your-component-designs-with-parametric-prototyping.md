# Enhance your component designs with parametric prototyping

Source: https://m3.material.io/enhance-your-component-designs-with-parametric-prototyping

Use AI tools to visualize your design explorations in real time


## Tab 1

When it comes to creating new components, like Material’s Expressive time picker (/m3/pages/time-pickers/overview), keeping track of design iterations and feedback in traditional UI software can be a real struggle. You’re often left with a design file that’s messy and inconsistent. Who hasn’t dealt with disorganized layers, broken components, or disjointed sketches after weeks of design reviews?
**Parametric prototyping makes the design process dynamic and efficient.** When you use interactive prototypes with flexible parameters instead of static drawings, you can visualize different design iterations in real time and easily refine ideas over the course of a project’s lifecycle.

#### From scattered to structured

The design process in tools like Figma or Illustrator often resembles a plot of scattered dots: Frames spread out everywhere. A massive grid of instances. Zooming around the canvas to find the right example.
**Prototyping with AI tools and using interactive sliders is a powerful way to visualize design changes on the fly.** Rather than fixating on a single setting and losing track of it within a sprawling design file, you can build a prototype that decouples variables such as color ranges, padding amounts, corner radius, or type size in code.
Using this approach provides a focused yet flexible space for you to explore. You can add new elements and immediately customize them in your prototype’s settings panel as you continue to refine the design.

#### When is it worth the effort?

Parametric prototyping works best for:

- Complex components that support a range of interactions, like Material time pickers (/m3/pages/time-pickers/overview), toolbars (/m3/pages/toolbars/overview), or tabs (/m3/pages/tabs/overview).

- Minor system updates, to compare old and new versions side-by-side.
