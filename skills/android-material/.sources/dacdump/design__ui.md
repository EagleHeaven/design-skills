# UI Design
Source: https://developer.android.com/design/ui

## Design for Android

Design beautiful and modern Android apps that meet your user where they are, whether browsing their phone, reading on their tablet, glancing at their wrist, connected to a display, or watching TV. [Get inspired by UX designs for a variety of screens →](http://developer.android.com/design/ui/gallery)

## Design for mobile

Create a beautiful and usable modern app design using Android themes, components, and adaptive layouts across phones, foldables, and tablets. [Explore mobile design →](http://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile) ## Enhance your app's productivity

Make sure your app is prepared with adaptive UI and interactions that support all desktop windowing and input methods. [Explore desktop design →](http://developer.android.com/design/ui/desktop) [Get inspired by UX designs for a variety of screens →](http://developer.android.com/design/ui/gallery) ## Boundless creation with XR

Create groundbreaking immersive experiences with powerful tools that transform how we interact with digital content and the world around us. [Design for XR headsets \& wired XR glasses →](http://developer.android.com/design/ui/xr)

## Extend your app for all-day wear

Create augmented experiences that feel like a natural extension of the user's perception, providing convenient access to information. [Design for intelligent eyewear →](http://developer.android.com/design/ui/ai-glasses) ## Enhance your app with widgets
