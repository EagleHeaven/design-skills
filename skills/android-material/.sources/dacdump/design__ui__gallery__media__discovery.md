# Media discovery and exploration
Source: https://developer.android.com/design/ui/gallery/media/discovery

<br />

Create a rich media feed that enables users to discover new content, new
artists, new recommendations and reviews, with more to see, more to choose from
on large screens.

Enhance the media viewing or listening experience in the expanded space of
large screens with a scrolling list of similar titles, published reviews, or
additional works by the same artists or actors.
