# Social media with dual screen display
Source: https://developer.android.com/design/ui/gallery/social/dual-screen

<br />

Display content on both screens of a foldable phone simultaneously. Take selfies
with the higher resolution rear camera. Make video calls with groups of people.
See what you say in different languages with a dual screen interpreter.
